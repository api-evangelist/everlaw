Meet Everlaw CFO Wesley Pua by Daniel Jubelirer Reading Time — 4 minutes August 20, 2026 Facebook LinkedIn This week, Everlaw welcomes Wesley Pua as its new Chief Financial Officer. Wesley brings more than 20 years of experience spanning finance and operational leadership at companies in aerospace, online education, ecommerce, and web hosting. Most recently, he served as CFO of Bluehost and Fabric, after holding senior leadership roles at Udemy and Planet.
