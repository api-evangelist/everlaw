Beyond Point Solutions: Why Unified Ediscovery Platforms Matter More Than Features by Gina Jurva Reading Time — 4 minutes July 23, 2026 Facebook LinkedIn For years, the London legal tech market has followed the same pattern. A legal team spots a problem, buys a tool to fix it, tries to wedge it into the existing technology stack, and spends months trying to persuade practitioners to actually use it. The result is a patchwork of disconnected tools that often forces firms to spend more time acting as part-time IT consultants than practicing law.
